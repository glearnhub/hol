A meeting counts when it is published or completed, has attendance recording switched on and its date is in the selected year. Cancelled events and drafts never count. Each person is counted once per meeting, even if checked in more than once, and registering without being checked in counts for nothing. People with the same number of meetings share a rank, and the next rank skips ahead (1, 2, 2, 4).
