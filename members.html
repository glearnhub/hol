Import members from a CSV or Excel file

  1. Download the template and fill in one person per row. Open it in Excel or Google Sheets. You can choose the finished file as it is (.xlsx) or saved as CSV.
    What goes in each column
    • Full name and Phone are required. 0712 345 678, 712345678 and +254712345678 are the same number.
    • Membership type: Incoming Commitment Class, In Commitment Class or Member. Leave blank for Member.
    • Gender: Female, Male or Other (F and M also work). Membership date: 2024-03-17 or 17/03/2024; blank means today.
    • Deaconess: her name exactly as it is in the Deaconesses list (capital letters do not matter).
    • Residence, Department, Alternative phone, Email, Church and Notes are optional.
    • The two sample rows in the template are ignored, so you can leave them in.
    • People who are already visitors are recognised by their phone number or email and made members automatically: their record, registrations and attendance stay, and the details in your file are added (anything you leave blank is kept as it was). People who are already members are never added twice.
  2. Choose your file. It is checked straight away and nothing is saved yet.
  3. Look at the result, then import. Nothing changes until you press the Import button.
